# 2. Daily 3-Hour Structure

| Subject | Daily Time |
|---|---:|
| Quantitative Aptitude | 60 min |
| Reasoning | 40 min |
| English | 40 min |
| General Awareness | 20 min |
| PYQs / Mistake Revision | 20 min |
| **Total** | **180 min / 3 hrs** |

### How to use each session

**Quant — 60 min**
- 20 min → Learn/revise concept
- 30 min → Solve questions
- 10 min → Review mistakes/formulas

**Reasoning — 40 min**
- 10 min → Concept
- 25 min → Questions/PYQs
- 5 min → Mistake review

**English — 40 min**
- 15 min → Grammar/vocabulary
- 20 min → Questions
- 5 min → Review mistakes

**GK/GA — 20 min**
- Learn/revise one focused topic
- Don't spend excessive time memorizing obscure facts

**PYQ/Mistakes — 20 min**
- Solve previous-year questions
- Maintain a mistake notebook

# 4. October 24 — Exam Day

## Before the exam

- Don't study for hours in the morning.
- Quickly review formulas/vocabulary if needed.
- Eat normally.
- Reach the centre early.
- Carry required documents/admit card.

## During the exam

Remember:

**+2 for correct  
-0.5 for wrong**

Don't blindly guess.

### Golden Rule

If a question is taking too long:

**Skip → move ahead → return later if time permits.**

# 7. Most Important Rules for These 19 Days

1. **PYQs > lengthy lectures**
2. **Practice > passive reading**
3. Analyze every mock.
4. Maintain a mistake notebook.
5. Don't try to complete 100% of the syllabus.
6. Don't spend 2 hours trying to master one difficult topic.
7. Focus on accuracy.
8. Start timed practice early.
9. Don't introduce major new topics after October 20.
10. Sleep properly before the exam.

## Final Goal

**57 hours of focused preparation → high-yield syllabus → PYQs → 3 full mocks → aggressive mistake correction → 150+ target.**
